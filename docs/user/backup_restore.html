Backup & Restore (.wfam)

On this page

Full System Backup & Restore (.wfam) ​

WealthFam provides a complete, portable System Backup & Restore workflow under Settings → Backup & Restore. You can export your entire family workspace into a single .wfam file before migrating servers, resetting an environment, or deleting an account—and restore it months later without depending on old server encryption keys or database IDs.

Who Can Use Backup & Restore ​

  • Allowed: OWNER and ADULT family members.
  • Restricted: CHILD and GUEST accounts cannot export, inspect, or import system backups.

What Is Included in a .wfam Backup ​

A .wfam archive captures all family-scoped data across WealthFam:

  • Family Circle & Workspace Settings: Tenant name, member profiles (email, name, DOB, PAN, phone, avatar, role), and workspace settings. (Login passwords, active sessions, and server encryption keys are never exported.)
  • Banking & Financial Instruments: Accounts, balance snapshots, credit card details, fixed & recurring deposits, provident funds (EPF/PPF/NPS), loans, and expense groups.
  • Ledger & Automation: All transactions (including transfer links and location coordinates), categories & subcategories, categorization rules, rule hit stats, budgets, and recurring transactions.
  • Investments & Goals: Mutual fund holdings, orders, SIPs, referenced scheme metadata, investment goals, and linked goal assets.
  • Statements & Document Vault: Statements, statement transactions, password cache, Vault folders, documents, version history, and actual Vault files (PDFs, receipts, thumbnails).
  • Ingestion & Notifications: Email sync configurations, sync logs, pending triage transactions, unparsed messages, custom parsing patterns, spam filters, ignored patterns, merchant aliases, and alerts.

Exporting a Backup ​

  1. Open Settings → Backup & Restore.
  2. Keep Encrypt backup with a custom passphrase enabled (recommended, enabled by default) and enter a strong passphrase (at least 8 characters) in both passphrase fields.
    • Inside the .wfam container, server-specific encryption is removed so the backup remains portable across servers. The outer .wfam file is then encrypted with your custom passphrase using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM.
    • If you explicitly disable encryption, the .wfam file is written without a passphrase lock—store it only in an encrypted location.
  3. Choose whether to include Document Vault files & statements (enabled by default).
  4. Click Download .wfam Backup and store both the file and your passphrase safely.

Restoring a Backup (Even Months Later or on a New Server) ​

  1. Sign in to WealthFam with an email address that belongs to the owner or any family member from the backup.
  2. Open Settings → Backup & Restore.
  3. Drop or select your .wfam file and enter the Backup Passphrase (if encrypted).
  4. Click Verify & Preview:
    • WealthFam decrypts the archive, validates its integrity, and displays the backup date, family name, record counts, and Email Verification status.
    • If your signed-in email does not match the owner or any family member in the backup, restore is blocked with a clear email mismatch error.
  5. Click Merge & Restore Backup:
    • Priority-Preserving Merge: Existing data on the new server is never wiped or overwritten. Existing accounts, categories, budgets, transactions, holdings, settings, and email configurations on the new server take highest priority, while missing records from the backup are merged in with freshly generated IDs.
    • All sensitive fields and Vault documents are automatically re-encrypted under the new server's active Tenant Data Encryption Key (DEK).
    • Any family members from the backup who don't yet exist in the new workspace are recreated automatically so account/holding ownership is preserved; update their passwords in Settings → Family Circle.
