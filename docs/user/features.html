✨ WealthFam Features ​

A comprehensive overview of what you can achieve with WealthFam.

📊 Holistic Dashboard & Unified Cash-Flow Math ​

  • Real-time Balance Sheet: Instantly view Net Worth, Liquidity, and Debt.
  • Spending Velocity: Sparklines and hierarchical category breakdowns powered by relational category_id foreign keys.
  • Unified Income, Expense & Investment Alignment: Across Web and Mobile, Operating Income strictly reflects 'income' categories, Living Expenses automatically net positive 'expense' refunds against gross debits, and internal transfers (is_transfer = True, exclude_from_reports = True) are universally excluded from analytics.
  • Full-Screen Interactive Spending Map (/spending-map & Mobile Full Map): Dedicated geo-intelligence screen with spend-tiered cluster-zoom (N · ₹sum), Hybrid/Pins/Heat layer switching, theme-aware basemaps, privacy-first location & merchant search (local matches + OpenStreetMap Nominatim place search), pin filtering (date, category, account, expense group, amount range), and live viewport spend summaries—backed by RFC 0001 encrypted coordinates.

📈 Mutual Funds Tracking & Intelligence Suite ​

  • XIRR & Performance: Automated tracking with Decimal precision.
  • Unified Portfolio: Manage multiple folios across CAMS, KFintech, and CDSL/NSDL CAS statements.
  • FIFO Tax Optimization & Harvesting: Real-time ₹1.25L annual LTCG exemption tracking (Finance Act 2024), FIFO lot decomposition, unrealized gains classification (LTCG vs STCG), and near-LTCG qualification alerts (315–365 days).
  • Portfolio Overlap & Entity Concentration: Pairwise fund overlap matrix $\sum \min(w_A, w_B)$ and aggregate company holding concentration with risk severity alerts and diversity scoring.
  • Sectoral Heatmap & Pareto Exposure: Exposure breakdown across 12 standard sectors, Large/Mid/Small cap allocation, and key constituent stock drilldowns.
  • SIP Intelligence & 30-Day Liquidity Calendar: Automated detection of recurring SIPs, verified vs auto-detected mandate management with one-tap toggle, cash-flow liquidity matching against primary bank balances, and 5/10/15-year compounding step-up simulator.
  • Peer Benchmarking & Risk-Return Quadrant Matrix: Cross-sectional Alpha ($\alpha$) vs Beta ($\beta$) quadrant matrix chart, Sharpe ratio analysis against category TRI benchmarks, and Glassmorphic AI Advisor executive intelligence reports.
  • Family Member Filter Parity: Seamless toggling between individual family member portfolios and consolidated household intelligence views.

🧠 AI Intelligence ​

  • Smart Categorization: Automated classification.
  • Deep Insights: Gemini-powered analytics.

🕒 Subscription Management ​

  • Recurring Transactions: Factor billing into liquidity.

🏦 Specialized Financial Instruments ​

  • Fixed & Recurring Deposits: Track deposits with automated compounding interest simulation, tenure timelines, and maturity projections.
  • Provident Funds (EPF / PPF / NPS): Tripartite EPF split (Employee, Employer, Pension), annual statutory interest estimation, and post-tax statutory deposit tracking (₹1.5L annual ceiling).
  • Credit Cards: Track statement cycles (current_unbilled, last_statement), utilization health, bill due dates, and minimum payments due.
  • Loans & EMIs: Manage lenders, track loan account numbers, and monitor EMI debits.

🔄 Cross-Account Transfers & Pairing ​

  • Automated Match Detection: Smart heuristic engine detects funds moving between your own accounts (e.g. Bank to Card bill payment or savings transfers).
  • Zero Expense Inflation: Paired transfers are automatically excluded from spending analytics, keeping cash flow reports accurate.
  • 1-Click Counterpart Leg Generation: Auto-generate missing receiving credits directly with adjustable amounts for cashback or payment discounts.

🔐 Zero-Knowledge Privacy & Enterprise Encryption ​

  • Field-Level Database Encryption (RFC 0001): User full names, email addresses, phone numbers, PAN numbers, account numbers, email IMAP passwords, and GPS transaction coordinates are encrypted at the database level with AES-256-GCM.
  • Encrypted Document Vault: All physical documents, tax filings, statement PDFs, and receipt thumbnails on disk (/data/vault/) are chunk-encrypted using streaming AES-256-GCM with magic header b"WFENC\x01". Stored files cannot be opened or read outside of authorized tenant sessions.
  • Blind Index Lookups: Exact-match searches (such as logging in with your email or linking accounts) operate via deterministic HMAC-SHA256 blind indexing, preventing DB dumps from exposing plaintext.
  • Zero-Retention Ingestion: Raw email and SMS bodies are processed statelessly in-memory and discarded immediately after extraction, eliminating long-term raw communication storage.
  • Consent-Driven Training Sharing: Users can voluntarily choose to share anonymized transaction patterns for parser improvements; shared items undergo format-preserving structural sanitization before transmission.

📂 Document Vault & Attachments ​

  • Persistent /data/vault Storage & Self-Healing: Documents persist across container upgrades in /data/vault with automatic startup/runtime path self-healing and surviving earlier-version fallback resolution.
  • Hierarchical Storage: Organize tax forms, receipts, statements, and investment documents in nested folders.
  • Transaction Linking & Lazy Previews: Attach receipts and invoices directly to transactions with in-app PDF and image previews, on-demand auto-unlocking of legacy encrypted statement PDFs, and lazy thumbnail generation without 404 console spam on locked PDFs.

📱 Mobile App (Android) ​

  • Real-time Ingestion: Automated SMS financial alert capture with high-accuracy GPS tagging.
  • Document Scanner with Auto-Straightening: Integrated native document scanner featuring auto-edge detection, perspective straightening, shadow removal, and contrast enhancement to capture and attach crisp receipt documents directly to transactions and Vault folders.
  • 3-Way Transaction Classification: Manual transaction entry seamlessly classifies outflows as Expense or Investment, or inflows as Income, with category scoping tabs (All, Expense, Income, Investment).
  • Unified Savings Rate & 6-Month Trend Sparklines: Mobile dashboard features smooth Bézier sparklines for 6-month investment and spending trends, with real-time unified savings rate calculations strictly separating investments from operational living expenses.
  • Attention & Activity Hub: Two-tab command center with actionable pending items (unlocked statements, triage items, card dues) and a persistent, deduplicated notification activity stream synchronized with the database.
  • Accessible Touch Target Compliance: Enforced $\ge 48\times 48\text{ dp}$ primary touch targets and $\ge 36\text{ dp}$ inline actions for effortless single-handed mobile operation.
  • Mobile Transfers Hub: Approve detected transfers in bulk and create counterpart legs on the go.
  • Dedicated Instrument Views: Instant mobile access to Deposits, Provident Funds, and Credit Cards.
  • Active Expense Group Assignment: Group assignment picker cleanly filters out archived groups while preserving historical assignments.
  • Living vs Total Spend Alignment: Ledger spend headers explicitly reconcile operational living spending against investments and internal transfers with clear tooltip breakdowns.

🏦 Bulk Statement Reconciliation & Management ​

  • Attended Statements Hidden by Default: Attended statements are hidden by default across Web and Mobile to keep review queues clean and focused on action items. A 1-click Show Attended / Showing Attended toggle reveals both attended and unattended statements whenever audit is needed, with real-time selection auto-advance upon attending.
  • Statement-Level Full CRUD: Upload or email-sync PDF statements, filter by status (All, Parsed, Pending, Failed) and filename, edit filename (synced with Vault), statement type, and linked account, and delete single or multiple statements with optional Vault PDF removal.
  • Statement Ignore Rules: Block recurring promotional/non-statement email PDFs by sender, email subject, or filename keyword directly from the Statements view (via the Ignored Rules manager or single-statement 🚫 Ignore modal) as well as Settings → Ingestions, with bulk cleanup of existing matching PENDING/FAILED items while keeping PARSED history safe.
  • Smart PDF & CDSL eCAS Ingestion: Specialized extraction for bank statements, credit cards, and Consolidated Account Statements (CAMS, KFintech, CDSL eCAS@cdslstatement.com, NSDL), SHA-256 version deduplication preventing duplicate Vault versions on retries, and partial-unlock reprocess continuity when decryption succeeds ahead of parsing.
  • Automated Rule Persistence: Generate classification rules directly from ingestion flows with PostgreSQL-safe JSON keyword queries.
  • Bulk Triage & Normalized Categories: Confirm hundreds of transactions in seconds with searchable category selection (:teleport="true" to prevent container clipping) and leaf-category normalization.
  • Pending Triage Auto-Promotion: Reconciling statement transactions automatically resolves matching pending triage items into official ledger records.
  • Category Rules Review Prioritization: Rules matching pending triage items are automatically pinned to the top of the rules list with amber badges (⚡ X in Review) and quick-filter toggles.

⚡ Global Attention & Action Required Hub ​

  • Unified Action Center: Top-bar unified Bell icon with pending badge count (🔔 {N}) on Web and dynamic "Pending Actions" row on Mobile aggregating all pending financial decisions into a single click-to-resolve queue.
  • Statement Attendance Lifecycle: Explicitly mark parsed statements as "Attended & Closed" or reopen them, with protective confirmation warnings when unreconciled transactions remain and auto-resetting upon reprocessing.
  • Multi-Domain Intelligence: Automatically detects pending triage transactions, suggested cross-account transfers, overdue or approaching credit card bills, category budget breaches, loan EMI deadlines, recurring subscription patterns, account low-balance/overdraft states, and stale mutual fund CAS statements.
  • Child Account Shielding: Automatic role-based security strictly hides household debts, loans, statements, and financial accounts from child members while preserving their personal triage queue.
  • Sub-15ms Single-Pass Aggregation: High-performance conditional aggregation engine ensuring instant UI responsiveness without background polling load spikes.

💾 Portable System Backup & Restore (.wfam) ​

  • Complete Server-Independent Export: Export your entire family workspace—accounts, transactions, categories, rules, budgets, loans, credit cards, FDs, PFs, mutual funds, goals, statements, Document Vault files, email configs, and settings—into a single .wfam file under Settings → Backup & Restore.
  • Custom Passphrase Encryption: Server encryption keys and database UUIDs are stripped for cross-server portability, then wrapped by default in a streaming 64KB chunked AES-256-GCM + PBKDF2-SHA256 container locked with your own passphrase.
  • Email-Verified, Priority-Preserving Merge Restore: Restore months later or on a brand-new server using the owner or any family member's email; existing data on the new server is never wiped and keeps highest priority while backup records merge in with freshly generated IDs.
  • Role-Protected Access: Available to OWNER and ADULT family members; CHILD and GUEST accounts are strictly restricted.
