💳 Financial Instruments & Transfer Engine Architecture ​

This document outlines the architecture, data models, services, and UX parity for specialized financial instruments and cross-account transfer pairing in WealthFam.

1. Domain Separation Overview ​

Prior to this architecture, credit cards, fixed deposits, and provident funds were treated as generic rows in the accounts table with generic metadata.

WealthFam implements a Domain-Separated Instrument Architecture where:

  1. accounts acts as the universal double-entry ledger root (maintaining running balance, tenant isolation, and ownership).
  2. Domain extension tables hold specialized instrument metadata and metrics (compounding frequencies, tripartite EPF shares, cycle dates, utilization).
  3. Specialized services (CreditCardService, FixedDepositService, ProvidentFundService) own instrument lifecycle, analytics, and business rules.
  4. AccountService remains 100% instrument-agnostic.

2. Supported Instrument Domains ​

A. Credit Cards ​

  • Table: credit_card_details
  • Model: CreditCardDetails (account_id, credit_limit, billing_day, due_day, card_network, card_tier, card_last_4, apr_rate, autopay_enabled)
  • Key Concepts:
    • Single Source of Truth: User-configured billing_day (1–31) and due_day (1–31) are strictly preserved.
    • Dynamic Billing Cycles: CreditCardService.get_cycle_transactions() supports segmenting transactions into current_unbilled, last_statement, and all.
    • Bill Payment: Dedicated endpoint records double-entry transfers from bank accounts to credit card liabilities.

B. Fixed & Recurring Deposits (FD / RD) ​

  • Table: fixed_deposit_details
  • Model: FixedDepositDetails (deposit_type, principal_amount, interest_rate, compounding_frequency, start_date, maturity_date, maturity_amount, monthly_deposit)
  • Key Concepts:
    • Compounding frequency math (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, Annual).
    • Accrued interest simulation up to the current wall-clock date.
    • Dynamic progress indicator percentage and days to maturity.

C. Provident Funds (EPF / PPF / NPS) ​

  • Table: provident_fund_details
  • Model: ProvidentFundDetails (fund_type, uan_number, pran_number, employee_share, employer_share, pension_share, annual_interest_rate, fy_contributions)
  • Key Concepts:
    • Tripartite balance breakdown for EPF (Employee share, Employer share, EPS Pension share).
    • Annual interest estimation based on official statutory rates (e.g. 8.25% EPF, 7.10% PPF).
    • Post-tax statutory FY deposit tracking against the ₹1.5L statutory ceiling (PPF / EPF voluntary contributions).

D. Loans & Liabilities ​

  • Enhanced with lender_name and loan_account_number for automated EMI detection and bank transfer linking.

3. Intelligent Cross-Account Transfer Engine ​

Transfers between internal family accounts (e.g., Bank → Credit Card bill payment, Bank → Wife's Bank, Bank → Loan EMI) frequently enter the ledger as two disconnected transactions.

Heuristic Discovery & Scoring ​

TransferDetectionService.scan_candidates() inspects unlinked outflows (amount < 0) and inflows (amount > 0) within a configurable lookback window (default 90 days):

  • Asymmetric Matching:
    • Exact match ($\Delta < ₹0.05$): High base confidence (75%).
    • Offer / Discount / Fee tolerance ($\Delta \le ₹500$ or ratio $\ge 0.90$): Flagged with descriptive reason.
    • Part-payments: When recipient narrative explicitly cites destination card mask.
  • Date Proximity: Same day (+15%), 1 day apart (+10%), within 4 days (+5%).
  • Identity & Text Correlation: Account name mentions, card mask matches ($*1234$), and family member names.

Human-in-the-Loop Approval & Parity ​

  • Zero Silent Merges: In accordance with enterprise audit standards, transfer matches are returned as suggestions for human review.
  • Atomic Linking: When approved (pair_transactions), linked_transaction_id is mutually populated, and exclude_from_reports = True is assigned to both legs to prevent artificial inflation of income or expense totals while preserving double-entry ledger balance.
  • Credit Card Dues Sync: Linking a transfer to a Credit Card automatically deducts the paid amount from total_amount_due and updates min_amount_due. Unlinking safely restores card dues.
  • 1-Click Counterpart Leg Generation: When an outflow is recognized as an internal transfer but the destination credit transaction is missing from statements, users can auto-generate the matching credit leg on the target account directly from either Web (Transfers.vue) or Mobile (transfers_hub_screen.dart), with adjustable credited amount for merchant discounts.
  • Batch Approvals: Both Web and Mobile feature 1-click batch approval for high-confidence matches ($\ge 80%$).
