📱 Mobile Native Architecture ​

WealthFam Mobile (Android) is the primary real-time ingestion sensor for the ecosystem. It is engineered to capture, hash, and transmit financial alerts with absolute reliability and minimal battery impact.

⚡ Native Ingestion Lifecycle ​

The app utilizes a dual-layer approach for SMS capture, integrating Flutter with low-level Android background services.

🛡️ Secure Capture & Permissions ​

To maintain a Precision audit trail, the app requires specific high-privileged permissions:

  • RECEIVE_SMS: High-reliability real-time listener.
  • READ_SMS: Used for historical sync and bulk scanning of older records.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Ensures the ingestion service completes the hash-and-send cycle even if the device enters deep sleep.

🔒 Security Principles ​

  1. Local ID Generator: Every transaction is assigned a unique UUIDv4 on the device before transmission.
  2. Privacy Masking: Personal identifiers (like full account numbers or OTPs) are filtered out at the native layer using strict regex ignore-patterns.
  3. Encrypted Transport: All data transmitted to the backend uses TLS 1.3 with pinned certificates (in production).
